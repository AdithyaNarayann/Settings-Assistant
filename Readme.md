# Settings Assistant

> **You know what you want. You just don't know where it is.**

Changing a phone setting should be simple.

But users often have to **remember technical names, search with the right words, or dig through layers of menus** just to find something they already know they want.

> `phone-il letters valuthakkanam`
> **→ Font Size**

The user knows the goal.
The phone knows the terminology.
**Settings Assistant bridges the gap.**

## What it does

Describe what you want in **Malayalam, Manglish, or English**.

```text
Your words
   ↓
Understand intent
   ↓
Find the setting
   ↓
Open the right control
```

No memorizing setting names.
No hunting through menus.
No asking someone where to find it.

### When you're not clear

> `Sound kurakkanam`

Could mean:

**Ringtone · Media · Alarm**

Instead of guessing, the assistant can **ask what you mean**.

## The Goal

> **Make phones understand what people want —
> instead of making people understand the phone.**
